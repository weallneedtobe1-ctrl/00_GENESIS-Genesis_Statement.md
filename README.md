Genesis Declaration

HGDP — Human Gross Domestic Product is proposed as a new economic theory and experimental framework for investigating how economic and technological systems can create, measure, verify, and release human value back to individuals and society.

The Genesis of the framework is recorded as:

9 September 2026

This date establishes the historical T0 reference point of the HGDP research program.

⸻

Core Proposition

GDP measures what an economy produces.

HGDP proposes to measure the verified human value that an economy releases back to humanity.

The purpose of HGDP is not to assume that more production automatically means greater human welfare.

Instead, HGDP investigates whether an economy can create greater human value while reducing unnecessary human time, energy, effort, waste, and conflict.

⸻

Genesis Architecture

The initial conceptual architecture is:

Human → Technology → Knowledge / Action → Measurement → Verification → Human Value → Public Value → HGDP

The first experimental pathway is:

One Human → One Smartphone → One Question → One Theory → Knowledge → Distribution → Application → Measurement → Verification → Human Value

T0 Human Value Event

The Genesis Event records the creation of the HGDP theoretical framework through the interaction of:

Human + Smartphone + AI + Knowledge Creation

At T0, the existence of the Genesis Event is documented.

However, quantitative benefits are not automatically considered verified.

The following remain subject to future measurement:

* Human hours returned
* Energy saved
* Unnecessary effort reduced
* Productivity gain
* Human capability gain
* Public value created

⸻

Evidence Principle

HGDP establishes the following distinction:

Potential Value ≠ Verified Value

A theoretical possibility is not the same as a measured economic outcome.

Creation Date ≠ Value Realisation Date

The date a theory, product, technology, or system is created is not necessarily the date on which measurable human or economic value is realized.

Device Energy ≠ Social Energy

The energy consumed by a device is not necessarily equivalent to the total social energy saved or consumed because of its use.

These distinctions must be tested through measurement rather than assumed.

⸻

Initial Research Objective

The initial objective of HGDP is to investigate whether human value can be measured systematically alongside conventional economic output.

The research asks:

Can an economy produce more human value while using less unnecessary human time, energy, and effort?

If measurable and independently verifiable, such information could complement conventional economic statistics and provide a different perspective on economic progress.

⸻

Experimental Status

This Genesis Statement does not claim that HGDP has already been scientifically validated.

It establishes the starting point for an open research program.

Future research may confirm, modify, reject, or replace parts of the framework.

All future findings should be recorded according to their evidence level.

⸻

Historical Integrity

The T0 Genesis Statement is a historical record.

Future versions may expand the theory and methodology but should not rewrite the historical Genesis event.

Any subsequent change must be identified through version control.
